How credentials are kept
  • Only in this server's memory, for your browser session – never written to disk and never sent back to the browser.
  • Your browser only holds a random session id (an HttpOnly cookie that page scripts can't read). Other people using this server have their own sessions and can't see or use your stacks.
  • Forgotten automatically after a period of inactivity, when the server restarts, or right away with “Disconnect” / “Forget all credentials”.
  • Use a management token scoped to what you need, and on a shared server keep .env without credentials.
